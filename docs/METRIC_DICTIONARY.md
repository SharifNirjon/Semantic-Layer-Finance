# Metric Dictionary

Generated by `python scripts/build_metric_dictionary.py` from `cube/model/cubes/*.yml`. Do not edit by hand.

**Business date = posting date** for transactions; snapshot cubes use month end; campaigns use the month the campaign started. Segment and branch are always as of the business date (SCD Type 2).

Time semantics: `flow` = summed over the period; `period_end` = value at the last month end of the period; `average` = mean of the monthly values in the period.

## accounts

### `accounts_opened` - Accounts opened

Accounts opened during the period.

* **Formula:** Accounts whose open date falls within the period.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.dim_account
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, product, status

## balances

### `total_deposits` - Total deposits (period end)

Closing deposit balances (savings, current, fixed deposit, DPS) at period end.

* **Formula:** Sum of closing balances of all deposit accounts at the last month end of the period.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category
* **Caveats:** Reconciled monthly to the general ledger (see Trust center).

### `avg_deposits` - Average deposits

Average daily deposit balance over the period.

* **Formula:** Sum of average daily balances of deposit accounts in each month; for a longer period, the mean of the monthly figures.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `average` (mean of the monthly values in the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category

### `casa_balance` - CASA balance (period end)

Current and savings account balances at period end.

* **Formula:** Sum of closing balances of Current and Savings accounts at the last month end of the period.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category

### `casa_ratio` - CASA ratio

Share of deposits held in current and savings accounts (low-cost funding).

* **Formula:** CASA balance divided by total deposits, both at period end.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category
* **Caveats:** A falling CASA ratio means funding is shifting to costlier term deposits.

### `interest_income` - Interest income

Interest earned on loans (recognised only on performing loans).

* **Formula:** Interest accrued on loan accounts in the period; interest on loans 90+ days past due is suspended.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category

### `interest_expense` - Interest expense

Interest paid on deposits.

* **Formula:** Interest accrued on deposit accounts in the period.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category

### `net_interest_income` - Net interest income (NII)

Interest income on loans less interest expense on deposits.

* **Formula:** Interest income minus interest expense over the period.
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category

### `nim` - Net interest margin (NIM)

Annualised net interest income as a share of average earning assets.

* **Formula:** Net interest income divided by average earning assets (the loan book), annualised (x12 over monthly averages).
* **Owner:** Chief Financial Officer
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_account_snapshot
* **Dimensions:** account_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, product_category
* **Caveats:** Earning assets are loans only; investments and placements are not modelled.

## campaigns

### `campaign_cost` - Campaign cost

Marketing spend of the campaigns in scope.

* **Formula:** Campaign cost (BDT), allocated evenly across targeted customers.
* **Owner:** CMO
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_campaign_response
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, campaign, campaign_channel, target_segment

### `campaign_response_rate` - Campaign response rate

Share of targeted customers who responded.

* **Formula:** Responders divided by customers targeted.
* **Owner:** CMO
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_campaign_response
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, campaign, campaign_channel, target_segment

### `campaign_conversion_rate` - Campaign conversion rate

Share of responders who converted to a product.

* **Formula:** Conversions divided by responders.
* **Owner:** CMO
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_campaign_response
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, campaign, campaign_channel, target_segment
* **Caveats:** Conversion is measured against responders, not against everyone targeted.

### `cac` - Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

Campaign cost per customer attributed to the campaign.

* **Formula:** Campaign cost divided by the customers attributed (converted) to the campaign.
* **Owner:** CMO
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_campaign_response
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, campaign, campaign_channel, target_segment
* **Caveats:** Attributed customers are responders who opened a product via the campaign.

## customers

### `total_customers` - Customers (period end)

Customers still with the bank at month end.

* **Formula:** Customers acquired on or before month end who have not churned by month end.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel
* **Caveats:** Point-in-time; a multi-month period returns the last month end.

### `active_customers` - Active customers

Customers with at least one transaction in the 90 days up to period end.

* **Formula:** Customers with a posted transaction in the 90 days ending on the last day of the period (period end).
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month, marts.fct_transactions
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel
* **Caveats:** Point-in-time; a multi-month period returns the last month end. Transaction dates use posting date.

### `new_customers` - New customers

Customers acquired during the period.

* **Formula:** Customers whose acquisition date falls within the period.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel

### `churned_customers` - Churned customers

Customers who left the bank during the period.

* **Formula:** Customers whose churn date falls within the period.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel

### `churn_rate` - Churn rate (monthly)

Share of opening customers who churned. Monthly rate; for longer periods it is the average monthly rate.

* **Formula:** Customers churned in the period divided by the sum of monthly opening customers. For one month this equals churned divided by customers at the start of that month; for a quarter it is the average monthly churn rate.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel
* **Caveats:** Always a monthly-equivalent rate, so months, quarters and years are directly comparable.

### `products_per_customer` - Products per customer

Average number of distinct product types held per customer.

* **Formula:** Products held divided by customers at period end.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** ratio
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month, marts.dim_account
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel
* **Caveats:** Counts distinct product types (savings, FD, card, ...), not accounts.

### `cross_sell_rate` - Cross-sell rate

Share of customers holding two or more product types.

* **Formula:** Customers with at least two distinct product types divided by customers at period end.
* **Owner:** Head of Retail Banking
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_customer_month, marts.dim_account
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, acquisition_channel

## loans

### `loans_outstanding` - Loans outstanding (period end)

Outstanding principal of the loan book at period end.

* **Formula:** Sum of outstanding principal of all live loans at the last month end of the period.
* **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_loans
* **Dimensions:** loan_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, sector, classification, aging_bucket
* **Caveats:** Reconciled monthly to the general ledger (see Trust center).

### `loans_disbursed` - Loans disbursed

Principal disbursed during the period.

* **Formula:** Principal of loans whose disbursement date falls within the period (loans still on the books at that month end).
* **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_loans
* **Dimensions:** loan_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, sector, classification, aging_bucket

### `npl_balance` - NPL balance (period end)

Outstanding balance of non-performing loans (Substandard, Doubtful, Bad-Loss).

* **Formula:** Outstanding on loans classified Substandard, Doubtful or Bad-Loss at period end.
* **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_loans
* **Dimensions:** loan_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, sector, classification, aging_bucket

### `npl_ratio` - NPL ratio

Share of the loan book that is non-performing.

* **Formula:** NPL balance divided by loans outstanding, both at period end.
* **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_loans
* **Dimensions:** loan_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, sector, classification, aging_bucket
* **Caveats:** Classification by days past due - Standard <30, SMA 30-89, Substandard 90-179, Doubtful 180-269, Bad-Loss 270+. Loans over 360 days are written off and leave the book.

### `provision_coverage` - Provision coverage

Total provisions held as a share of NPL balance.

* **Formula:** Total provisions (all classes, demo rule) divided by NPL balance at period end.
* **Owner:** Chief Risk Officer
* **Time semantics:** `period_end` (value at the last month end of the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_loans
* **Dimensions:** loan_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, product, sector, classification, aging_bucket
* **Caveats:** Simplified demo provisioning rule, not the regulatory schedule.

Number of posted transactions.

* **Formula:** Count of transactions with a posting date in the period.
* **Owner:** Head of Digital Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** count
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_transactions
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, channel, direction, product

### `txn_value` - Transaction value

Total value of posted transactions (debits and credits).

* **Formula:** Sum of transaction amounts (BDT) with a posting date in the period; debits and credits both counted.
* **Owner:** Head of Digital Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** bdt
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_transactions
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, channel, direction, product

### `digital_share` - Digital share of transactions

Share of transactions made through mobile or internet banking.

* **Formula:** (Mobile + internet transactions) divided by all transactions, by count.
* **Owner:** Head of Digital Banking
* **Time semantics:** `flow` (summed over the period)
* **Unit:** percent
* **Source tables:** marts.fct_transactions
* **Dimensions:** customer_key, business_date, branch, region, segment, channel, direction, product
